# Access Control Policy

Version: 1.0
Effective date: June 1, 2023
Owner: IT Department

Status: SUPERSEDED by Access Control Policy v2.0, effective March 1, 2026. Retained for audit history only.

## Purpose

This is the original Quillmark Access Control Policy, as it stood when it was issued. It set the early rules for who may use Quillmark systems and how that access is kept in check. It is no longer in force and is kept so that auditors and managers can see what the requirements were during the period it applied.

## Scope

The policy applied to employees and contractors who used Quillmark systems and to the accounts that gave them access. It covered the systems run by the IT department and the applications that staff used for company work.

## Roles and responsibilities

The IT department owned this policy. It created and removed accounts, carried out the access reviews described below, and kept the records. Managers told the IT department when a person joined, changed role, or left. Employees and contractors were responsible for keeping their own credentials private and for reporting anything suspicious to the IT department.

## Policy

### Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for remote access only. The IT department set up multi-factor authentication for people who needed to connect from outside company networks. Other access to Quillmark systems relied on a password alone.

### Access reviews

User access reviews are performed semi-annually, every six months, by the IT department. The IT department compared the accounts on each system with the list of current staff and asked managers to confirm that the access still matched each person's work. Access that was no longer needed was removed by the IT department, and the outcome of each review was recorded.

### Leavers

When a person leaves Quillmark, all of their access is revoked within 72 hours of their last working day. Managers were expected to tell the IT department in advance so that the removal could be carried out on time.

### Passwords

Passwords must be at least 10 characters long and must be changed every 90 days. People were asked not to reuse a password from another service.

## Enforcement and review

Breaches of this policy were handled by the person's manager together with the IT department, and serious breaches were referred to the People team. The policy was reviewed by the IT department when it saw a need.

This document has been replaced and is not updated. Anyone looking for the access rules that apply today should use the current Access Control Policy.
