# Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Version: 2.0
Effective date: January 15, 2026
Owner: General Counsel

## Purpose

This policy states how long Quillmark keeps the information it holds and how that information is destroyed when the time comes. Keeping data longer than there is a reason to creates risk for customers and for Quillmark, and destroying it too early can break legal and contractual duties. The policy sets a clear rule for each main kind of data so that neither happens by accident.

## Scope

The policy covers customer data, backups, application and audit logs, and employee records, in every system and location where Quillmark holds them, including systems run for Quillmark by vendors.

## Roles and responsibilities

The General Counsel owns this policy and is the only person who can place or release a legal hold. System owners are responsible for making sure that deletion happens on the systems they run and for keeping the evidence of it. The Platform Security team provides the approved deletion methods and checks that they work. The People team manages employee records. The Security team reviews the deletion register and raises anything that looks incomplete.

## Policy

### Customer data

Customer data is deleted within 30 days after a customer contract terminates, unless a legal hold applies. Deletion covers the data in the production platform and in any working copies, exports, or test environments that hold it. System owners confirm that no copy has been missed before they record the deletion as complete.

### Backups

Backups are retained for 35 days and then permanently deleted. Backups are kept only to recover from failure and are not treated as an archive. A backup that has reached the end of its retention is deleted even when keeping it would be convenient.

### Logs

Application and audit logs are retained for one year. Logs are kept so that incidents can be investigated and so that access to systems can be shown to auditors. Logs are protected against change while they are held, and they are deleted when the retention period ends.

### Employee records

Employee records are retained for 7 years after an employee leaves. The People team holds these records in the HR system and restricts access to those who need it for a defined purpose. When the period ends, the records are deleted in the same way as other data under this policy.

### Deletion and verification

Deletion means that the data can no longer be read or recovered by ordinary means or by Quillmark's own staff, using a method the Platform Security team has approved for the type of storage involved. Each deletion is recorded in the deletion register, and customers may request a certificate of deletion. The register shows what was deleted, from which system, when, and who carried it out. A certificate is issued by the General Counsel's office from the register entries.

### Legal holds

Only the General Counsel can place or release a legal hold, and a legal hold suspends scheduled deletion. A hold applies to the data named in it and to nothing else, and the General Counsel tells the owners of the affected systems when it begins and when it is lifted. Once a hold is released, the suspended deletion resumes, and the register records the hold and the deletion that follows it.

## Enforcement and review

Retaining data past the periods in this policy, or deleting data that is under a legal hold, is a breach and is handled under the Information Security Policy. Anyone who is unsure whether data may be deleted must ask the General Counsel before acting.

The General Counsel reviews this policy on the schedule set by the Information Security Policy and whenever a change in law or in a customer contract affects the periods it states. Changes are approved by the Security Steering Committee.
